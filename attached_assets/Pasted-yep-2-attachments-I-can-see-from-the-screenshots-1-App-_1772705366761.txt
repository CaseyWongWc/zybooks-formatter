User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0 (X11; Linux x86_64) AppleWebKit/537.36 ...`

The zyBooks server validates that requests look like they're coming from the real frontend. Without those headers, it rejects with 400. ~5 lines of code, then the whole pipeline should light up.
